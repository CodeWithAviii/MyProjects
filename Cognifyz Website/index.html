About the Program

A comprehensive internship designed to transform students into industry-ready developers

Our Web Developer Internship at Cognifyz Technologies is a 6-month immersive program designed for students and recent graduates who are passionate about building the digital future. This isn't your typical internship - you'll be working on real client projects from day one.


Throughout the program, you'll rotate through our core development teams, gaining exposure to various aspects of web development including frontend, backend, and DevOps. Each intern is paired with a senior developer mentor who will guide you through challenging projects and help accelerate your learning.


The internship includes weekly training workshops, code reviews, and opportunities to contribute to open-source projects. Our goal is to provide you with the skills, experience, and professional network to excel in your tech career.


Program Duration: 6 months (Full-time)

Start Dates: January, June, and September

Location: Remote & On-site options (Bangalore HQ)

Desired Qualifications

We're looking for passionate developers with these skills

Technical Skills

  • Proficiency in HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Experience with frontend frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue)
  • Understanding of backend technologies (Node.js, Python, or Java)
  • Knowledge of databases (SQL or NoSQL)
  • Familiarity with RESTful APIs
  • Version control with Git and GitHub
  • Responsive design principles

Bonus Skills

  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  • Knowledge of DevOps practices and tools
  • UI/UX design principles
  • Testing frameworks (Jest, Mocha, Jasmine)
  • Progressive Web Apps (PWA)
  • Serverless architecture
  • Containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)

Personal Attributes

  • Strong problem-solving abilities
  • Eagerness to learn new technologies
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Ability to work in a team environment
  • Self-motivated and proactive attitude
  • Time management and organization skills
  • Portfolio of personal or academic projects
